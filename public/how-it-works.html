Getting Your Hospital on MediMonitor is Simple

Three steps to a fully digital patient monitoring operation — up and running in under 24 hours.

01

Register Your Hospital

Contact us to set up your hospital account. We configure your admin panel, beds, and wards within 24 hours.

02

Add Your Staff

Your admin creates accounts for doctors and nurses in minutes. Each gets a personalised dashboard immediately.

03

Start Monitoring Patients

Nurses register patients and log vitals. Doctors receive real-time alerts. Admins oversee the whole hospital live.

Who Does What on MediMonitor

Every staff role has a dedicated, purpose-built dashboard.

Administrator

  • View all patients hospital-wide
  • Add doctors and nurses
  • Monitor bed occupancy live
  • Track critical alert cases

Nurse

  • Register new patients
  • Allocate beds and assign doctors
  • Log and update vitals
  • Manage assigned patient list

Doctor

  • View assigned patients by specialization
  • Receive real-time emergency alerts
  • Review full patient vitals history
  • Discharge patients when ready
