Everything Your Hospital Needs in One Platform

Purpose-built tools for hospital administrators, doctors, and nursing staff — working together seamlessly.

Real-Time Vital Monitoring

Track heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and temperature live. Instant alerts when readings go critical.

Smart Bed Management

Automatically assign and track bed occupancy. Nurses allocate beds and doctors with a single click — zero paperwork.

Automated Emergency Alerts

Critical vitals trigger instant audio-visual alarms on the responsible doctor's screen — every second counts.

Role-Based Access Control

Separate secure dashboards for Admins, Doctors, and Nurses. Each role sees only what they need.

Digital Patient Records

Full patient history, vitals logs, medication notes, and discharge records stored securely and retrievable instantly.

Admin Analytics Dashboard

Hospital administrators get a live bird's-eye view of occupancy, critical cases, and staff activity at all times.

Real-Time Socket Updates

Powered by Socket.io — every vital update, alert, and bed change is pushed live to the right person instantly.

Discharge History Logs

Full audit trails of every patient's journey — from admission through treatment to discharge — always accessible.

Secure Authentication

Role-based login ensures only authorised personnel access patient data, with session management built in.
